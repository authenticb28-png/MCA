Modern Computer Architecture
Samajh ke Padho

CSA222 · all 15 units · samjho kya ho raha hai, kaise ho raha hai, phir numerical khud solve karo.

Ye notes alag kyun hain: Pichle notes mein "one-hot" jaise words bina matlab ke likhe the. Yahan har mushkil word ka aasaan matlab, ek chhota example ke saath, wahin pe diya hai jahan woh pehli baar aata hai. Har topic pehle bataata hai kyun chahiye, phir kya hai, phir kaise kaam karta hai (0/1 values ke saath), phir exam jaisa sawal step by step solve karke dikhata hai.

Har topic is order mein hai:

Pehle socho: ye cheez kis problem ko solve karti hai, ek roz ki zindagi wala example.
Definition: exam mein likhne wali English definition.
Mushkil shabd, aasaan matlab: har technical word ka matlab, chhote example ke saath.
Kaise kaam karta hai: numbered steps, koi step skip nahi.

Diagram kaise padhein: diagram mein pehle kya dekhna hai, aur ek value daal ke trace.

Formula: har symbol ka matlab ke saath.
Solved example: exam jaisa sawal, har line ka calculation, aur last mein Answer.
Galti mat karna: jo galti students sach mein karte hain.

Trick: yaad rakhne ka hook.

Ek line mein: pura topic ek sentence mein.

Har unit ke end mein "Exam mein kya aata hai → kaise solve karein" table hai: question type aur uska step-by-step method.

Kaise padhna hai (1 din mein):
  1. Samjho: "Pehle socho" + "Kaise kaam karta hai" padho. Har step pe khud se poocho "ye kyun?".
  2. Haath se karo: solved example ka sawal padho, answer dhak do, khud solve karo, phir steps se match karo. Padhne se zyada yaad tab rehta hai jab khud nikaalte ho.
  3. Bol ke dekho: "Ek line mein" box ko dhak ke topic apne shabdon mein 30 second mein bolo. Atak gaye to wahi part dobara padho.
  4. Diagram ek baar banao: must-draw diagrams copy pe ek baar haath se banao.
  5. Raat ko: sirf "Ek line mein" boxes, unit-end tables aur last page ka cheat sheet.

Ye tareeka learning research se liya hai: Feynman technique (bina jargon ke samjhao), worked examples (pehle solved example dekho, phir khud karo), dual coding (words + diagram saath mein), aur retrieval practice (dhak ke khud yaad karo). Content class slides aur labs ke hisaab se hai, Mano (Digital Design), Harris & Harris (Digital Design and Computer Architecture) aur Patterson & Hennessy (Computer Organization and Design) se check kiya hua. Units 14 aur 15 ki class slides nahi thi, woh textbooks se hain.
